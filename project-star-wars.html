Introduction
This project explores form, function, and storytelling through a Star Wars droid concept—developed from early sketches through refinement and presentation visuals.
Early exploration focused on silhouette, readability, and believable function—finding a shape language that felt at home in the Star Wars universe.
The Vision
Create a droid concept that feels iconic, practical, and story-ready.
Final composition and polish focused on clarity and presence—making the concept feel “real” through lighting, material cues, and storytelling details.
