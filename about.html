Jared Teiger

I build stories you can hold in your hands.

I’m fascinated by the process of turning imagination into something tangible; from sketch to prototype, from pixels to production.

My journey began at Georgia Tech, where I learned to think critically about how design, art, and engineering come together. Since then, I’ve explored it all: 3D printing, sculpture, animation, and design visualization. I’ve helped take products from concept to full-scale manufacturing, and I continue to push my curiosity into new creative mediums.

Spacer Robotics

I partnered closely with the founding team to shape Spacer Robotics’ early product strategy, researching construction-site workflows, defining user needs, and co-designing next-generation robotic concepts that aligned form, function, and brand vision. I created the company’s visual identity, produced a three-minute investor pitch video from storyboard to final edit, and engineered a functional 3D-printed prototype that integrated mechanical, electrical, and iterative design to validate core mechanisms and support fundraising efforts.

Autodesk

Led hands-on Fusion workshops, introducing students to 3D modeling and real-world design. Strengthened ties between Autodesk and Georgia Tech’s maker community. Built communication skills by adapting to diverse learners and fostering a collaborative environment.

Helloreal

Launched PSVR2 adapter, expanding product line into VR. Designed 25+ concepts and 15+ Fusion-based prototypes to test clasp and lock mechanisms. Optimized prints for efficiency. Supported marketing by starring in and editing a Tennis Pro Swing promo video.

Actasys Inc.

Rendered and animated visuals of Actajet tech for CES 2023 and client outreach. Created 10+ infographics and videos explaining sensor-cleaning products. Designed a thermal vent mock-up and filmed a promo video during CES for social media use.

Panspatial Aerospace Concept Design

Designed Trillium Stargate aircraft and Epiguard Shuttle for portfolio and pitch assets. Researched flight systems to guide modeling decisions. Created Argonas rail weapon for Modern Warship, ensuring accurate form, function, and visual detail across all assets.
